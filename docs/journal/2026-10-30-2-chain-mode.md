## What it does

`#voice-test` → mode **chain**: four calls to say in one go. Each is ticked green as it's named.
Pausing between calls gives each one its own latency.

- **The chain is one a caller could call** (`src/voice/chain.ts`). From a Static Square, each call
  is Standard where the one before left the dancers, by square-one's `STANDARD_APPLICATIONS`, and
  each shows the formation it ends in. Only applications with a known ending, said as the call's
  name or with who it's for ("heads square thru four", "centers run"). A `who` that's a variant or
  a fraction is skipped. Example from the browser: *heads square thru four → pass to the center →
  double pass thru → first couple go left* (Eight Chain Thru → Double Pass Thru → Completed Double
  Pass Thru → Facing Lines).
- **It moves the square and looks ahead.** Calls that change the formation are tried first; no call
  repeats; a step is only taken if four calls can still be finished from where it ends. Some
  formations have no sayable call with a known ending (Inverted Square, the thars, tidal lines),
  and the first version stopped there at two calls. 88% of steps move the square (seeded, 200
  chains). Most of the rest come after Alamo Style, where every call that can go on keeps the Alamo
  Ring.
- **Every call in the transcript, in order** (`matchCalls` in `match-call.ts`). Left to right, the
  longest spelling where one starts, else skip a word.
- **Each call keeps the moment it was first named** as the interim grows. If the recogniser
  revises a call, it and everything after are re-timed from the revision.
- **Segments.** The level meter's pauses split the utterance. With one segment per call, each call
  gets *end of its segment → its naming*. Otherwise only the last call's *end of speech → named*.
- **Summary, chains:** heard exactly; calls heard in order (longest common subsequence, so one miss
  costs only itself); per-call latency; last call; final. Chain trials stay out of the one-call
  summary. Results JSON is `version: 2` with `chainSummary`, and trials carry `chain`, `calls`,
  `segments`, `carried`.

## The carry-over, fixed

The first run's early matches came from Safari sending the previous final again: once on its own
before the meter heard speech, and again at the front of the next final ("Star through right left
through"). Both are dropped before matching now (`dropCarry`), in every mode. The cost: if the meter
misses the start of a repeated identical call, that call is dropped too.
