## What was built

- **`figurePaths(call, "couple" | "pair", shapes, sizing)`**: any `#review` figure's floor paths
  with no browser. A couple figure is `couplePaths`, as before. A facing pair's (Dosado, Pass
  Thru, Allemande Left) is danced as `useDancePerformance` dances it: `applyCallToPair`, sized by
  the pair's clearance and grip radius. Each comes with **the engine's grips**. `couplePaths` and
  pair calls share one sampler.
- **`whole-move-figure.ts`, `figureMove`**: any figure as a `Move`. The twirl is `twirlMove`.
  Hands-free figures get no hold. Anything held another way returns `unsupported` with the
  reason.
- **`Move.stepCap`**: past it, the beau's step costs ten times clipping, with an exact gradient
  (`smoothCost`). At clipping's own weight a twirl still stepped 4 cm past a 10 cm cap.
- **`solve-all.ts`**: jobs split over worker processes (this script again with `--shard=i/n`);
  `results.json` (metrics and variables) and `report.md`, outliers first. Defaults: every figure
  but the twirl, a 10 cm cap, 16 workers. `--figures`, `--pairings`, `--cap=none`, `--workers`,
  `--out`.
- **`pathFrames`** lifted out of `twirlMove` so both builders share it.
- `whole-move-figure.test.ts`: a pair's capture, the forearm grip refused, the cap holding on a
  real solve.
