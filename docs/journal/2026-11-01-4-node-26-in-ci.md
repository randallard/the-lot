## What changed

- `docs/adr/1020-…` is copied in, and `docs/adr/1013-…` has its Status flipped. Both match the
  template byte for byte, and so do the index rows.

  no `renovate.json`, so 1020's Renovate hold on `@types/node` doesn't apply.
