## A bug: sound matches weren't marked `~`

"starred", "traded" and "passed" have the same sound key as "star", "trade" and "pass", so their
distance was 0, and `~` was shown only for a distance above 0. `HeardCall` and `Named` now carry
`exact`; the screen marks every match that wasn't an exact spelling. An exact spelling also beats a
sound at the same distance. 37 voice tests.
