Where it stops

The hands don't stack — they splay, 0.19 apart on the worst pairing. With hold.forward near zero each arm reaches purely across to the shared wrist point, so the hands continue across in opposite directions past it. Your sketch has both arms reaching mostly forward, which is what puts the hands over each other.

And hold.forward can't just be raised. It's already maximal under ADR-0027 — "as if the upper arm is relaxed and hanging straight down" — which pins the elbow directly below its own shoulder and spends forward only on what's left after across and down.
